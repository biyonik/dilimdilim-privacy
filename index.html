Privacy Policy

How we handle information while your child explores fractions, one slice at a time.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version

No account is required. Profile names, progress, and learning records stay on your device. We do not operate a game-data server or use a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

The Android app can show non-personalized ads through Google AdMob and offers an optional purchase through Google Play. These services process some information, as explained below.

01 About this policy

Dilim Dilim is an educational food-truck game designed for children ages 6–11. The app is developed and operated by Ahmet Altun ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy covers the app and this privacy policy page.

If you have questions, a parent or guardian can contact us at ahmet.altun60@gmail.com.

02 Information stored on your device

The app stores the information it needs to run the game and show parents their child's progress:

  • Local profiles: profile identifiers and display names entered by a parent. A nickname is sufficient; a child's real name is not required.
  • Game progress: completed levels, stars, virtual tips, decorations, and selected items.
  • Learning records: answers, accuracy, fraction skills, practice history, and time spent in the app, used for local learning reports and practice suggestions.
  • Settings and purchase status: language, sound and music preferences, parental time limits, advertising frequency settings, and whether the Parent Pack is owned.

Profile names, progress, and learning records are stored in encrypted on-device storage. We do not upload these records to a server, send them to advertisers, or synchronize them between devices. The developer cannot remotely access them.

03 Children's privacy

The app does not ask children to create online accounts or provide an email address, phone number, date of birth, or postal address. It has no chat or public profiles and does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, or precise device location.

Purchases, profile management, parental settings, and external links from the app are protected by a parental gate. Ads are requested with child-directed treatment and non-personalized advertising settings.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us so we can address the concern and delete information we do not need to retain.

04 Advertising and third-party data

The Android app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between completed levels. Ads are not shown during active gameplay. Owning the Parent Pack removes ads.

We configure ad requests for child-directed treatment, users under the age of consent, non-personalized ads, and a maximum ad content rating of G. The Android app removes permissions for the Advertising ID and Android AdServices advertising identifiers.

Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. When the advertising service is used, Google may collect or receive:

  • An IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location.
  • Device and app information, including permitted device or account identifiers, such as the app set ID, where available under the child-directed configuration.
  • Interaction information, such as app launches, ad impressions, taps, and video views, and SDK performance or diagnostic information.

Google processes this technical information to deliver and measure ads, operate its services, and help prevent fraud. We do not provide Google with local profile names or learning records. For details, see Google Mobile Ads data disclosures and Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or another separate analytics or crash-reporting service. This does not remove the advertising SDK's own technical data processing.

05 In-app purchases

The Parent Pack is an optional, one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment and associated store-account information under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your card details.

The app receives product and transaction information from the store to check, grant, and restore the purchase. Purchase ownership is stored locally and checked with Google Play. Restoring a purchase does not restore local profiles or learning progress.

06 Sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Local profiles and learning records are not shared with third parties. Google processes advertising and purchase information as described above and may process that information in countries outside your own, under its privacy policies.

Profile names, progress, and learning records use AES-256 encryption at rest. Google's Mobile Ads SDK encrypts transmitted data using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

07 Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local profiles, game progress, settings, and aggregate learning results remain on your device until the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Daily learning and play-time history is limited to the most recent 60 recorded days per history category; older entries are removed as new days are recorded.

  • View or correct: use the parent area to view learning reports and change profile display names or parental settings.
  • Delete local data: clear Dilim Dilim's storage in Android device settings or uninstall the app. This removes local profiles and progress.
  • Remove ads: purchase the optional Parent Pack through the parent area.

We cannot retrieve or delete device-only records remotely. Clearing app data does not delete Google's purchase records or information already processed by Google. Google's retention practices and available privacy controls are described in its privacy policy.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information and to object to or restrict certain processing. A parent or guardian can contact us for help with information we hold, such as support correspondence, or contact Google regarding information it holds.

08 When you contact us

If a parent or guardian emails us, we receive the sender's email address and the information included in the message. We use it to respond and resolve the request, and retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations.

Please avoid including a child's real name or other identifying information in support messages. Local learning records do not need to be sent to us to use the app.

09 This policy page and external links

This page does not add cookies, analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party fonts. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to serve the page and protect its services. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before introducing new data practices.
